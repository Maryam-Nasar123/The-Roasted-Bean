Our Story

A Century of
Good Coffee

✦

Nestled on the corner of Elm & Third since 1923, The Roasted Bean began as a humble roasting house where travellers and locals alike gathered to share stories over a slow‑poured cup. Three generations later, that same warmth fills every corner.

We source our beans directly from small‑batch farms across Ethiopia, Colombia, and Sumatra — roasted in‑house every Tuesday and Friday. Our kitchen turns out recipes handed down on worn index cards, baked fresh before dawn.
